For clients

What METHOD
provides

Before the review, you agree on the deliverable version, acceptance conditions and sufficient evidence. An independent reviewer runs the checks and records what could be established.

You receive a report with a conclusion for the reviewed version, evidence and reproduction steps. Use it to decide whether to accept the deliverable, request corrections or collect missing information.

Recipients of the complete report are agreed in advance. They receive it regardless of its conclusion or the payer's position.

Reviews are led by Madi Baimuratov, the author of METHOD. The basis for the conclusion is available for rechecking within the agreed scope. Identified errors in a report are examined and corrected.
